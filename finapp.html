Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RRFinEApp GST compliant? +

Yes, fully. RRFinEApp generates GST-compliant Sales and Purchase invoices with auto CGST/SGST/IGST computation. GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B ready reports can be exported as Excel, JSON, or CSV, ready to upload directly to the GST portal.

Do I need to install anything? +

No installation whatsoever. RRFinEApp is 100% cloud-based. Just open your browser, log in at fin.rrsindia.co.in, and you're ready. Works on any device, Windows, Mac, or even a tablet.

Is my data safe? What about backups? +

Your data is stored securely on AWS Managed PostgreSQL, with encryption and automated daily backups. You can also keep your own copy: back up to your personal Google Drive on a schedule you choose. Posted entries are immutable, so no one can silently edit or delete a confirmed entry, and every user action is covered by full audit trails with SuperAdmin health monitoring.

I use Tally. Will I find it familiar? +

Absolutely. RRFinEApp is designed to feel familiar to Tally users. Keyboard-first navigation with arrow keys, F2 for New, F5 to Refresh, Esc to go back, and Dr/Cr entry format, just like Tally, but in the cloud.

Is RRFinEApp an alternative to Tally, Busy, SAP or Dynamics 365? +

Yes. RRFinEApp is built as a modern cloud alternative to Tally, Busy, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and SAP. It keeps the familiar Dr/Cr accounting and keyboard-first speed of Tally and Busy, adds the multi-company, role-based access and audit trail you expect from an enterprise ERP, and runs fully in the cloud at SME-friendly pricing.

Can I manage multiple companies? +

Yes. Multiple companies under a single login with FY date enforcement per company. CA firms and businesses with multiple entities find this especially useful.

How do I get started or request a demo? +

Contact us via the Contact form. We'll set up your demo account and walk you through the application, usually within the same day.

I am a CA or accountant. Can I manage many clients? +

Yes. The Professional Full Suite lets a CA, accountant or tax firm manage many client companies from a single login, with role-based access for your team and a dedicated professional portal.

Can I give my accountant access to my books? +

Yes. You can grant your accountant or CA scoped access to your company for the period you choose, and withdraw it any time. Every action stays on the audit trail.

Can I move my data from Tally or Busy? +

Yes. You can bring in your opening balances and item details through a simple Excel upload, so you can start mid-year without re-entering history. We are happy to help you through it.

Do you help with setup and training? +

Of course. We set up your account, walk you and your team through the app, and stay with you as you get going. Our team is always a message away.

Is there a free trial? +

Yes. You get a free demo with full access for 30 days, extendable on request, so you can try RRFinEApp properly with your own data before you decide.
