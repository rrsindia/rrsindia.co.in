Introducing RRFinEApp

Cloud-based financial accounting built for Indian SMEs and CA firms. Like Tally or Busy, but in the cloud. Like D365 or SAP, but simple.

📒

Full Accounting

General Ledger, Cash Book, Bank Book, Trial Balance, Day Book, complete double-entry accounting.

🧾

GST Invoicing

GST Sales & Purchase invoices with auto CGST/SGST/IGST. GSTR-1 & 3B ready exports.

📊

Final Accounts

P&L, Balance Sheet, Ageing Reports, real-time, formatted for Indian businesses.

☁️

100% Cloud

No installation. Access from anywhere, any device. Stored on AWS Managed PostgreSQL with daily backups, plus optional backup to your own Google Drive.
