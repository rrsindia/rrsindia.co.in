Who We Are

R.R. Sphere India

A technology and learning company with over 30 years of IT expertise — bridging the gap between enterprise-grade software and the practical needs of Indian SMEs, CA firms, and learners.

Our Story

Built on Experience

R.R. Sphere India was founded in 1995 in Ludhiana, Punjab — starting with computer education and training. Over three decades, we evolved into a full-service IT company delivering custom software, AI-powered cloud solutions, and career-focused coaching.

Our flagship product RRFinEApp is a cloud-based financial accounting system — bringing the best of Tally's familiarity and Microsoft D365's power to Indian businesses at an affordable price.

We believe technology should be accessible, practical, and built for real-world Indian business needs — not adapted from foreign platforms. Everything we build is made for India, by India.
