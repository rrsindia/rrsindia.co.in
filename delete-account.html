🗑️ Account & Data Deletion

Delete your account & data

Your data is yours. Here is exactly how to have your RRFinEApp account and its data removed, in plain language.

📩 How to request deletion

RRFinEApp accounts are set up for organisations, so deletion is handled as a verified request. To delete your account and its data, do either of the following:

  • Email rrsphere@rrsindia.co.in from your registered email address, with the subject "Delete my RRFinEApp account", or
  • Raise a request on our Support page.

We confirm the request comes from the account owner before we act, so that no one else can delete your books.

🧹 What gets deleted

On deletion we permanently remove the personal and business data held in your account, including:

  • Your profile and login details (name, email, phone)
  • Your financial records (accounts, items, invoices, vouchers, ledgers and reports)
  • Registered devices and their sign‑in tokens
  • Approximate location and device information kept for the audit trail

📦 Export first, if you wish

Deletion is permanent. If you want a copy of your books before they are removed, export your data from the app first, or ask us and we will help you take it with you.

🗂️ What we keep, and for how long

Indian tax and company law require some financial records to be retained for a fixed period. Where that applies, a minimal set of records may be held securely, used for nothing else, and then permanently deleted once the statutory period ends. Routine backups are purged on their normal rotation cycle.

We do not sell or share any retained data, and it is never used for advertising.

⏱️ How long it takes

We action verified deletion requests within 30 days. We will confirm by email once your account and data have been removed.

Questions about your data? See our Privacy promise or contact us any time.
