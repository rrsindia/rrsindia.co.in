🔒 Trust & Security

Your data is safe with us

Your books are precious. We protect them the way we would want ours protected: in India, encrypted, backed up every day, and seen by no one but you and the people you allow.

☁️ Where your data lives

RRFinEApp runs on the Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud, in the Mumbai region, so your data stays in India. It sits in an AWS Managed PostgreSQL database with managed updates, so the platform is looked after around the clock.

How we keep it secure

Every layer is built to protect your information, all the time.

🔐

Encrypted connections

Your data travels over secure, encrypted links, every time you log in and work.

👤

Role-based access & 2FA

You decide who can see and do what. Add two-step verification for an extra layer of safety.

📜

Full audit trail

Every change is recorded, so you always know who did what, and when.

💾

Daily backups

Your data is backed up every day, automatically, so nothing is ever left to chance.

⏱️

Point-in-time recovery

If ever needed, your data can be brought back to a previous point, not just the last backup.

✅

Restores we test

A backup only matters if it works. We test our restores, so recovery is real, not just on paper.

🛡️ Shared cloud does not mean shared data

Your data is kept separate from every other customer and is fully access-controlled, so no other customer can ever see it. This is how trusted cloud software works the world over, and it is exactly what lets us bring you enterprise-grade features at a price that makes sense.

Your data is yours. You can export it any time. We use it only to run the service for you, and we never share it with other customers or sell it to anyone.

⚡ Always on, always improving

We treat your uptime as our responsibility. RRFinEApp runs on reliable cloud infrastructure and is monitored so it stays available when you need it. New features and improvements ship without interrupting your work, and any rare maintenance is handled with care.

⭐

Need a dedicated setup?

For businesses that need their own private database, we offer a dedicated option as a premium add-on. Just ask.
