📄 Terms of Use

Simple & fair terms

How we work together, in clear language. No traps, no surprises, just a fair understanding between you and us.

🔑 Your account is yours to look after

Keep your password private and your login to yourself. You are responsible for what happens under your account, so let us know straight away if anything looks wrong.

📊 Your data, your responsibility

The data you enter stays your property, and the accuracy of what you enter is in your hands. We are here to keep it safe and to help you make the most of it.

🧾 Reports are tools to help you

GST returns, financial statements and other outputs are there to make your work easier and faster. Please give them a final check before you file or rely on them, just as you would with any good tool.

✅ Honest, lawful use

Use RRFinEApp for your own genuine business accounting (or, for professionals, for the clients you are authorised to act for). Please do not try to misuse or disrupt the service.

📅 Your subscription

Your plan gives you access for the period you have signed up for. To keep enjoying RRFinEApp, simply renew when your period is ending. Pricing may be updated from time to time, and we will always be upfront about it.

🔧 We keep it running and improving

We work hard to keep the service available and to make it better with every release. Now and then a little maintenance may be needed, and we handle it with care so it stays out of your way.

🤐 Confidentiality, both ways

If you are given access to someone else's books (for example as a professional), please keep that access confidential and use it only for the agreed work. We hold ourselves to the same standard with your data.

Questions about anything here? Get in touch, we are happy to explain. See also Privacy and Trust & Security.
