🤝 Privacy

Your data is yours

No jargon, no fine print games. Here is exactly how we treat your information, in plain language.

📝 We collect only what we need

To run your account we keep your basic details (name, business, contact) and the financial data you enter. We also keep a record of changes for your own audit trail. That is it. We do not gather anything we do not need to serve you.

🇮🇳 Your data stays in India

Everything is hosted on the AWS cloud in the Mumbai region. Your information stays within India.

🚫 We never sell or share it

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with other customers or advertisers. Ever. We use it for one purpose only: to provide the service to you.

👁️ Only you, and who you allow

Your books are visible only to you and the people you give access to. Our team looks at your data only when you ask us for help, and only as far as needed to help you.

📤 Yours to take or remove

You can export your data whenever you like. If you ever close your account, your data goes with you and is removed from active use.

🍪 Just enough cookies

We use cookies only to keep you securely signed in and to make the app work. No tracking for advertising.

Want to know more about how we protect your data? See Trust & Security, or contact us with any question.
